My 2026 Recap

Boston Summer Trip

My trip to Boston started on Thursday morning and ended on Saturday evening. My friend and I took the train, which was quite uncomfortable but cheaper than the flight. We stayed with our childhood friend, who moved to the Boston Suburbs. My overall experience was great. Boston is a beautiful, old, and lively city.

Chicago Summer Trip

My trip to Chicago was for work. It was my first time in the city. I only got to explore a small part of the city, but it was beautiful. My work friends and I went to all the tourist spots like Cloud Gate. We had plans to try deep-dish pizza, but unfortunately, we did not eat any pizza. I will definitely plan a personal trip to Chicago in the future.
